AdvanCare

Affordable care plans, telehealth, non-emergency transport and caregiver booking for Cagayan barangays: a resident app, a staff console for LGU teams and an AI care assistant.

  • React
  • Capacitor
  • Hono on Workers
  • Neon Postgres
  • Drizzle
  • Gemini assistant

  • Staff console and resident app The LGU overview counts who is waiting for a caregiver, what is in progress, escalated and billed. Beside it, the resident app.

  • Book a doctor from home Residents pick a service, choose a day and time, and book any available telehealth doctor in Cagayan.

  • RHU visits, triage and prescriptions Request a Rural Health Unit visit, describe symptoms for clinician triage, or refill a prescription.

  • Transport, medicines and help Book non-emergency transport to CVMC, order medicines at member prices and ask the care assistant.

  • Requests reach the staff queue A resident’s triage request lands in the service request queue with who asked, when, and who is assigned.

  • Shifts board Caregiver shifts for the day, from needing a caregiver to confirmed, in progress and completed, with clock-in times.

MedCore

Applies PhilHealth, HMO and senior/PWD deductions in order so cashiers and security see one final balance. A staff dashboard, a patient companion app and an AI concierge.

  • Vercel dashboard
  • Expo
  • Hono on Workers
  • Neon Postgres

  • Hospital dashboard and patient app Billing staff see discharge holds, pending HMO authorizations, bed census and receivables at a glance. The patient sees the balance left after coverage.

  • Discharge clearance Every discharge moves through six stages, from the doctor’s order to the gate pass, with the current blocker and the time elapsed against the 45-minute target.

  • One bill, every deduction in order Staff see each charge and how VAT exemption, senior or PWD discount, PhilHealth and HMO are applied. The patient sees the same breakdown on their phone.

  • A bill patients can understand Patients check their bill and coverage, and Cora AI explains the balance in Taglish, English, Tagalog or Ilokano.

  • Claims checked before they are filed A pre-flight scrubber checks PhilHealth claims before submission. Patients can settle the balance in full or in part from the app.

  • Revenue cycle analytics Average discharge time, trapped capital and the main bottleneck, with an AI briefing on what to fix first.

SERVIO

Services sold like catalog items: upfront prices, instant technician matching, live on-the-way tracking and in-app escrow. Launching in Tuguegarao City.

  • Ionic
  • Angular
  • Capacitor
  • Hono on Workers
  • Drizzle
  • Postgres
  • Durable Objects

  • Home and live tracking Book a verified helper, then follow them on a real street map of Tuguegarao from accepted to arrived, and message them in the app.

  • Verified pros, upfront prices Profiles show NBI clearance and TESDA credentials. Checkout shows the fixed price held in escrow, and any extra part needs your approval with photo evidence.

  • Paid only when the job is done Escrow is released only when you share your completion PIN after inspecting the work. Then rate the helper and leave a tip.

  • Neighbors helping neighbors Neighbors post requests to the bulletin, browse helpers by skill and offer their own services at clear rates.

MedRec

Upload records, get plain-language AI readings, share them by QR and keep a Medical ID, rebuilt for v2 on a serverless stack.

  • Expo
  • Cloudflare Workers
  • Neon Postgres
  • R2 storage
  • Gemini

  • Medical ID, records and a share pass The Medical ID keeps blood type, allergies, conditions and an emergency contact. Records list newest first, and a timed QR pass shares only the records you pick.

  • Ask Remy and a health summary Ask Remy finds records in plain language, here the latest blood test. The health summary turns conditions, medications and recent results into one short page.

  • Sharing and profile Pick records, set a time limit and send a link; active and past shares stay listed. The profile holds health details, privacy settings and record exports.

A multi-page publication platform with article submission workflows, category hierarchies and role-based editorial access, backed by Supabase with row-level security.

mulat-media.vercel.app
