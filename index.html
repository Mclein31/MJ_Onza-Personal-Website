  • Fig. 2.1 of 7 Hospital dashboard and patient app Billing staff see discharge holds, pending HMO authorizations, bed census and receivables at a glance. The patient sees the balance left after coverage.

  • Fig. 2.2 of 7 Discharge clearance Every discharge moves through six stages, from the doctor’s order to the gate pass, with the current blocker and the time elapsed against the 45-minute target.
