# M6 — Phone layout report (Recognition & Training)

## Files changed
- `css/recognition.css`
- `css/drawer.css`
- `js/drawer.js`
- `index.html` untouched (all mobile layout/text reductions scoped to `@media (max-width: 700px)`, leaving desktop `>= 861px` identical)

## Removed / hidden on phones (`<= 700px`)
- **Recognition**:
  - `.strip` (horizontal thumbnail strip below Recognition that clipped at the right edge and duplicated photos already inside the index).
  - `.idx__copy h3` inside open accordion panels (redundant with the row title directly above).
- **Training**:
  - First generic boot-camp paragraph (`.training__body p:first-child`), leaving the single short MedCore/awards paragraph.
  - Secondary row subtitle (`.idx--training .idx__sub`) and duplicate panel metadata (`.idx--training .idx__meta`), plus `.idx__copy h3`.
  - Duplicate `.training__text` bottom border and excess gap above the Training index rows.

## Changed
- **Recognition & Training scroll clearance (`<= 700px`)**: Added `scroll-margin-top: calc(var(--nav-clear) - var(--pad-top))` on `#recognition` and `#training` and `scroll-margin-top: var(--nav-clear)` on `.idx__row` so anchor jumps clear the `60px`-bottom fixed nav (`labelTop` at `129–158px` across `320–430px`).
- **Compact editorial index rows (`<= 700px`)**:
  - Recognition `.idx__row`: two-line compact grid (`num` | `title` | `mark` over `date · sub`), `min-height: 44px`, `padding: 13px 0`, removing the bottom border on the expanded row so it flows directly into its open photo and single paragraph (`15px`).
  - Training `.idx--training .idx__row`: single-line compact row (`num` | `title` | `date` | `mark`) with `min-height: 44px` (`44px` measured), `padding: 11px 0`, and full-width `.training__img` for the open row.
- **Accordion scroll compensation (`js/drawer.js`)**: Changed `window.scrollBy(0, drift)` to `window.scrollBy({ top: drift, behavior: 'instant' })` so `html { scroll-behavior: smooth }` does not animate scroll compensation when collapsing a row above the tapped one.
