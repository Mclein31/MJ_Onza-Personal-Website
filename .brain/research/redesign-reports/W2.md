### Ticker band (`index.html` after the hero, `css/hero.css`, `js/hero.js`)
`<div class="ticker band--night" data-ticker aria-hidden="true">`: Python ✦ SQL ✦ PostgreSQL ✦ Pipelines ✦
Full-stack ✦ Cloudflare ✦ in Geist 900 cream, red four-point stars (CSS `clip-path`, no glyph dependency).
A composited WAAPI loop (~110px/s). Scrolling nudges `playbackRate` (down speeds it up, up runs it backwards)

### Signal line (`js/signal.js`, `css/signal.css`)
- One path through every visible `[data-signal-anchor]` in document order. Cubic S-bends with vertical
  tangents and handles at half the drop, so a bend never bulges outside the band between two anchors.
- **Layering:** every direct child of `<main>` becomes `[data-signal-host]` (`:where()` position relative +
  `isolation: isolate`) and gets its own `<svg class="signal">` at `z-index:-1`: above the section background,
  under its content. Each SVG holds the same document-space path and its `viewBox` shows its own slice.
- **Gaps:** when a pinned stage (`.work.is-pinned [data-work-pin]`, `[data-years].is-pinned`) sits between
  two anchors, the path splits into runs that dive into the stage top and resurface from its bottom.
- **Drawing:** the tip rides 62% down the viewport, eased. Once the hero has landed (`.hero.is-in` + 650ms) the
  first stretch flows out of the red full stop. Cream packets (pooled `<rect>`s, 10 per section) flow along
  the drawn part and shrink into the tip.
- **Side hints** `data-signal-side` steer into page-margin lanes beside the content column. An anchor already
  on its hinted side keeps its exact spot (W6's button end, W4's right edge).
- **Perf:** geometry is read only on ResizeObserver(main) / resize / font load / `load` (120ms debounce),
  through `offsetTop` chains, so reveal transforms do not skew it. The curve is sampled once into Float32 lookup
  tables. Each frame does binary searches + dash offset and packet transform writes on on-screen sections
  only. One rAF loop for line, packets and cursor ink, and it sleeps when idle. Headless frames averaged
  16.6ms.
- Reduced motion: full static line, no packets, no ink. Print: hidden.
- **Cursor ink** (canvas in the hero, fine pointer + hover, not reduced motion): red trail that thickens with
  speed and fades into the page colour within 1.2s. It fades by colour rather than alpha, so round caps do
  not bead.

## Known issues
- **The signal line is being rebuilt by another run** (owner: it "looks fake"). Per the orchestrator I stopped
  editing `js/signal.js` / `css/signal.css`. What the next owner should know:
  - Anchors placed by W3/W4/W5 are mostly 1px blocks or unpositioned absolutes at the container's left edge,
    whatever their side hint. That is why `signal.js` has the lane logic.
  - Under reduced motion (and short landscape phones) Journey is not pinned, so one S-bend spans the whole
    unpinned Journey (~4000px) and crosses slide captions diagonally.
  - Stack, Recognition and Activity crossings go behind opaque cards and over headings; not re-checked
    against "never cross body copy" after the last lane change.
  - On phones the line (18px) is wider than the 16px margin, so it sits flush with the screen edge and
    overlaps 1–2px of edge text.
- `tests/timing.spec.mjs` measures `.hero__name .char`, which no markup produces (already true before this
  run), so its result is meaningless. W7 should measure the `.ln > span` reveal instead.
- `tests/spacing.spec.mjs` expects `.hero` < 850 at one size, and `responsive.spec` expects `.hero` == viewport
  above 1100px. The latter holds; check the former against the new phone heights (708–811px).
- Dark theme: the black ticker sits next to the dark Work section; only W1's 1px inset edge separates them.
