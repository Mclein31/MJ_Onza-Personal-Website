# R1 — Recognition and Training editorial index

## What changed
The file-drawer metaphor (red slab, four-colour folder tabs, "DRAWER 01" bar) is gone from both sections. Both now use one shared component, the **editorial index**.

- **Desktop (from 64rem / 1024px)**: two columns. Left, a numbered list (mono index, mono date, Geist 800 title, one meta line, hairline rules). The active row has a red index numeral and a filled red marker with ink text; the others are muted with a hollow ring. Right, a sticky featured panel (top = nav height + 40px): photo at its natural aspect, 16px corners, no coloured frame, then title, description, venue and date, and the existing `VIEW PHOTO ↗` action. All panels share one grid cell, so switching never shifts the layout.
- **Narrow (phones and tablets under 1024px)**: calm accordion. One item open at a time, first open by default, photo and text inside. Tapping the open row closes it. The tapped row stays put under the finger while the row above collapses.
- **Interaction**: hover, focus or click on a row features it (160ms opacity crossfade; instant under reduced motion). Rows are real `<button>`s with `aria-current="true"` (plus `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls`). Arrow keys, Home and End move focus. Hover reacts only to real pointer movement, so rows scrolling under a resting cursor do not swap the panel (this was also what made the second `.award__img` click in `interactions.spec` flaky).
- Red is an accent only: index numeral and marker. No red background anywhere in these sections. Both themes use tokens only.

## Facts kept
Every title, description, venue, date, alt text, image `width`/`height`, `data-lb` index and `data-training-lb` hook is unchanged, and the `.award__img` / `.training__img` classes remain. All five recognition items remain, including "With High Honors". Row labels reuse the old tab labels (short title, category, date).
