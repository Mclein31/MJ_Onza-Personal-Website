# H2 — Hero Sizing (Phones & Tablets) & Journey Footer Nudge

## What Changed

### 1. Hero sizing & spacing (`css/hero.css`)
- **Desktop full-screen rule (`min-width: 1101px`)**: Removed `, (min-width: 961px) and (hover: hover) and (pointer: fine)` from `.hero__inner { min-height: 100svh; }` so `961px–1100px` viewports (including `961×1280`, `1032×1376`, `1100×1400`) keep the `min-height: min(100svh, 960px)` cap while `> 1100px` remains exactly `100svh`.
- **Phone & tablet stack (`@media (max-width: 60rem)`)**:
  - Reduced `.hero__inner` `padding-top` from `calc(var(--nav-clear) + var(--space-xl))` (`128px`) to `calc(var(--nav-clear) + var(--space-md))` (`104px`), placing `.hero__kicker` within `[88px, 120px]` below the floating pill nav.
  - Removed `.hero__mark { padding-top: clamp(32px, 7svh, 88px); }` to eliminate the empty band between `.hero__labels` and `.hero__name`.
  - Tightened `.hero__inner` and `.hero__mark` `gap` to `clamp(16px, 2.5svh, 28px)` and updated `.hero__bend` `top` offset to `calc(-0.5 * clamp(16px, 2.5svh, 28px))` to stay centered in the gap.
  - Scaled `.hero__name` down from `min(24vw, 11rem)` to `min(21vw, 9.5rem)`.
  - `.hero__i::after` and `.hero__period::after` use `em` offsets relative to `.hero__name`, so the red round i-dot and full stop remained aligned at all tested sizes.

### 2. Journey progress footer on `320×568` (`css/journey.css`)
- Added `@media (max-width: 960px) and (max-height: 640px)` to set `.years.is-pinned .years__sticky` `padding-top: var(--nav-clear)` (`88px`) and `padding-bottom: var(--space-md)` (`16px`), pulling `.years__foot` fully inside the `568px` viewport (`571.80px` → `555.80px`).
