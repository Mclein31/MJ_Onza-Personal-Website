## What changed
- **Brush look.** The line is now a filled outline, not a stroked tube. Width swells through curves,
  tapers to a point at both ends, the edges wobble slightly, and a few dry-brush streaks (nonzero-wound
  holes) are denser toward the tail. The cream packets are gone.
- **Routing.** The build step measures text lines, images, controls and cards in `<main>` with reveal and
  pin transforms taken out. Text of 40px or more counts as display type, so the line may pass behind it.
  A top-to-bottom shortest path runs on a coarse grid: cost rises near content, and `data-signal-side`
  hints pull the line across between sections on desktop (ignored below 861px). The path is then
  simplified and smoothed by corner cutting, and a corner is locked wherever rounding it would touch content. Width is clamped to the
  clearance at every sample. Phones get a thinner stroke (half-width 3.2 to 6.5px) that stays in the gutter.
- **Pinned stages.** The line is continuous through both pinned sections. In the Work deck and the Journey
  year stage, the stroke enters at the stage top, follows a lead-in to a loop placed in the largest free
  area (the union over every card position or slide, below the nav), and leaves by a lead-out routed
  around the stage's final layout. The loop is an ellipse that turns 1.6π with pin progress and draws
  as the deck moves. The lead-out draws only after the pin ends, so moving cards never cross it.
- **Drawing.** The stroke is still drawn by scroll, with the tip 62% down the viewport, and a round brush
  tip leads the drawn part. Reduced motion shows the complete static line (neither stage is pinned then).
- **Performance.** There is still one rAF loop, and geometry is built only on resize, load or font load.
  Each frame toggles static 420px outline pieces, rebuilds one tip outline, and rebuilds the stage curve
  only while a stage is on screen. Nothing reads layout per frame.
- **Cursor ink.** Each stroke is one filled brush shape that swells with speed and dries to a point
  (no more beaded round caps).
