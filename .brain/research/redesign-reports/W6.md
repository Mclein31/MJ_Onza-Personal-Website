# W6 — Contact + footer

**Status: built, NOT visually verified.** The dev-server command (`python3 -m http.server 4206`) was denied by the permission classifier, so no screenshots were taken and no Playwright run happened. Only `node --check` passed on the JS. Needs a visual pass at the section 7 device sizes, in light and dark.

## Changed
- `index.html` (`#contact`, message `<dialog>` title only):
  - `<section class="contact band--night">`.
  - New `.contact__head` row holds the headline `So, what are we <em class="serif">building?</em>`. It moved out of `.contact__lede`, and `data-scrub` and `.hl` were dropped from it.
  - Signal anchors: `data-signal-side="left"` beside the headline, and `data-signal-side="right"` inside the `Send a message` button, so the line ends at the form entry.
  - `.contact__side` wraps the idcard and the new copier markup. The wallet's four cards and their markup are unchanged.
  - Dialog title is now `Send a <em class="serif">message</em>`.
- `css/contact.css`: local night re-map (same logic as `.band--night`, with fallbacks, so it works before or after W1 lands), giant headline, red `avail` dot, idcard, tiles, and the restyled message dialog (night panel, red top rule, mono labels, red send pill).
- `css/wallet.css`: surfaces, type and accents moved to cream, red and ink. Mail is cream with a red top rule, LinkedIn is darker cream, WhatsApp is signal red. **The GitHub pass and its green contribution palette are untouched.** Personality animations are unchanged.
- `css/copier.css`, `js/copier.js`: CSS-drawn copier with a top slot, scanner lid, red COPY key and LED. Any `[data-copy]` click prints a slip with the address in mono. The slip prints in 12 steps over 900 ms, the COPIED LED stays lit for 2.6 s, and the slip retracts after 7 s. Under reduced motion the slip appears instantly. A `role="status"` `aria-live` region announces "Email address copied. A slip printed: …".
- `js/sfx.js`: new `print` sound (stepped printer ticks plus a ding). `[data-copy]` plays it when the copier exists, and the old `success` sound otherwise.
- `css/footer.css`: only accent colours moved. The wordmark's last letter, link hover, the vista `accent` layer and `--v-red` now use `--color-signal`/`--color-signal-text`. `js/vista.js` needed no change.
- `js/wallet.js`, `js/contact.js`: no change needed.

## Notes for other workers
- **W1:** contact tokens use `--color-night`, `--color-night-ink`, `--color-night-muted`, `--color-signal`, `--color-signal-ink` and `--color-signal-text`, each with a hex fallback. `em.serif` is also styled locally in contact.css.
- **W2:** anchors are absolutely positioned zero-size `<i>`s. The end anchor sits at the right of the `Send a message` button.
- **W4 (`js/ui.js`):** the clipboard write and toast still live in ui.js's `copyButtons()`. copier.js only listens for the same click, so keep the `[data-copy]` hook.
- **W7:** the `.contact [data-message-open]` selector still matches exactly one element. `.pass__copy[data-copy]` is still unique, because the machine's key is `.copier__btn`.

## Known risks (unchecked)
- Side-by-side layout (idcard plus 204 px copier) only applies above 1100 px. Below that the copier stacks under the card, right-aligned.
- Slip address is 11 px mono in a 204 px slip, so it may be tight at a 25-character address. It has `overflow-wrap: anywhere` as a fallback.
- The footer sits directly after the night band. If W1's footer background stays cream, the cut will be hard by design.
