## Motion (`scripts/design/work.js`)
On `gallery:change` with reason button / dot / key: the new screenshot wipes in with `clip-path` (left to right for
next, right to left for prev, wraps use the button's direction) over the outgoing one (a background-image ghost),
with a 2px orange scan line riding the edge; caption fades up. 380ms ease-out, a new change cancels the running one,
nothing under reduced motion. Swipes and mouse drags keep the native snap movement (no wipe).
To make the press a jump instead of a slide, without editing core, work.js wraps that one track element's
`scrollTo` and forces `behavior: 'auto'` only right after a prev / next / tick press or arrow key (capture listener).
Also toggles `.is-past` on ticks.
