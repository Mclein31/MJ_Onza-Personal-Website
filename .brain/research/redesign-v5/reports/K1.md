# K1: design-neutral content skeleton

File: `prototypes/skeleton/index.html` (generated once with a throwaway script; the file is hand-editable). Nothing else touched.

## What it is
Semantic HTML5 page, all inventory content in page order: head (title, description, canonical, robots, google-site-verification, OG, Twitter, theme-color x2, favicons, JSON-LD copied verbatim from `index.html`), skip link, header (brand, nav to all 8 sections, theme toggle, "Let's talk"), 8 sections, footer (copyright, nav, stats), 3 dialogs, toast. 84 `<img>`, all with width/height, decoding=async, lazy except the first hero tool icon (Python). Root-absolute asset paths, no inline styles, no old CSS/JS links. Review-only `<style>` in the head (system-font reset).

## Hooks (also listed in a comment at the top of the file)
- `data-theme-toggle`, `data-message-open` (header, hero, contact), `data-message-close/form/submit/status`
- `data-gallery` (+ `data-gallery-track`, `-prev`, `-next`); slides are `button[data-lightbox="<project id>"]` wrapping `img[data-title][data-desc]`
- `data-lightbox="recognition|training|<project>"`; recognition rows use `data-lightbox-target="recognition:<n>"` (n = index in the 8-photo strip)
- lightbox dialog: `data-lightbox-dialog`, `-image`, `-title`, `-desc`, `-count`, `-prev`, `-next`, `-close`
- `data-video-open` + `data-video-src` / `data-video-poster` / `data-video-title` (on the "Watch the product video" button and on the video slide); dialog `data-video`, `data-video-player`, `data-video-title-el`, `data-video-close`
- `data-activity` (`data-state="loading|ready|error"`), `-calendar`, `-loading`, `-error`, `-retry`, `-stats` (`data-act="total|days|streak|best|…Note"`, `data-hl`), `data-act-tip`, `data-activity-mini`, `data-activity-mini-total`
- `data-schema` + `tr[data-join]`, `data-copy="<email>"`, `data-stats-visits`, `data-stats-like`, `data-stats-likes`, `data-year`, `data-accordion`, `data-toast`, `data-toast-text`, `data-countup="7"`
