# Report H2: hero header redesign (one grid, recruiter strip)

Owner feedback on the live hero: (a) "line arrangement is not equal" (2555px dark screenshot), (b) "change header layout to
something better for looking for internship, do not put school and course" / "something more relevant should be on that header".

## Root cause of (a)
Measured on the old build at 2560 wide (`H2/before-2560-dark.png`, `before-1280-dark.png`):
- The hairline columns, the strip and the name live in `.container` (max-width 2400px, centred, so 80px of extra margin at 2560),
  but `.hero__plot` and `.hero__axis` were full viewport width and the ticks were at fixed `14% / 50% / 84%` of that. So at 2560
  Extract sat at x=387 (between lines 1 and 2 at 303 / 499), Transform at 1280 (line 6), Load at 2123 (between lines 9 and 10 at
  1866 / 2061): stage gaps 893px vs 843px, none of them on the 12-column grid. The WebGL stream and the SVG poster used their own
  hard-coded fractions (0.12 / 0.47 / 0.53 / 0.86) as well.
- The strip rule and the toolchain rule were `--rule`, the column hairlines `--rule-2` and the ticks `--ink-3`.
- The name sat 4px inside its column start (optical `-0.045em` pull) while everything else sat `--g/2` (10-12px) in.

## What changed
**One grid.** Every vertical line and text edge now derives from the `.container` grid (12 columns, 4 on phones, margin `--m`,
gutter `--g`, same 2400px cap, `100%` of the container, never `100vw`, so a scrollbar cannot skew it). A column line sits at the
gutter centre: line k = box-left + `--m` - `--g/2` + k * (column + `--g`).
- `.hero__plot` is now the container box (`width:100%; max-width:2400px; align-self:center`), so canvas, labels and hairlines share
  one frame. Outer margins are equal at every width.
- Stage labels are placed by CSS on the lines themselves: `left: calc((100% + --g) * k / n - --g/2)` with `--l12` 2 / 6 / 10 (or
  `--l4` 1 / 2 / 3 on phones). The old 1x8 tick is replaced by a 5px square on the line (Transform's is orange, the pinch point);
  the label's ground interrupts the hairline behind it.
- All hero hairlines use one token, `--hair` (= `--rule-2`), 1px, including the strip rule and the toolchain rule, which now run from
  the first to the last column line (they were full container width and `--rule`).
- Strip cells are 4 x 3 columns (2 x 2 on phones): their left edges are lines 0, 3, 6, 9, so the column hairlines *are* the cell
  dividers. Cell text, the name, the intro, the buttons and the readout are all `--g/2` inside their lines. The name and intro are
  pulled by the side bearing of their first glyph (Geist 600: M 0.08em, O 0.043em, I 0.08em) so the *ink* sits on the same edge
  rather than the glyph box.
- Phones: the hairline set is 4 columns (extra `<i>` hidden, right outer line drawn from the fourth), grid lines 5, stage ticks on
  lines 1, 2, 3.
- WebGL (`scripts/design/hero.js`): the vertex shader takes `uStage` = x of the three ticks (read from the `.hero__axis` label
  centres, so shader and labels cannot disagree): scatter until Extract, pinch onto Transform (plateau +-0.02), fan out to the lanes
  by Load. Settle targets are computed from the grid's tracks (`grid-template-columns`, gutter, padding), and the orange points now
  settle on the centre line (Transform) instead of an arbitrary line 7. The hero loop already redraws on resize.
- Static SVG fallback: regenerated from the same shader maths (`H2/gen-hero-poster.mjs`) for the new stage lines, plus
  `hero-field-{grey,orange}-phone.svg` for the 4-column grid (swapped in `@media (max-width:720px)`). Viewbox 1440x480 (was 1440x340)
  so the dots stretch less. The "N points · flow" readout is hidden in fallback (it was reporting "0 points" or a stale count on a still).

**Recruiter strip (b).** Program / University / Class of / Looking for are replaced; school and course no longer appear in the hero
(they remain in About). All four use only facts already on the site:
| Label | Value | Source on the page |
|---|---|---|
| Open to + static orange square | Data engineering internships | Journey "Looking for my first data role"; footer "Open to internships and projects" |
| Focus | SQL · PostgreSQL · Python · ETL | Journey "SQL and PostgreSQL first, Python for data, then ETL and orchestration" |
| Shipped | 7+ projects, web and mobile (link to #work) | About "Shipped 7+ projects"; Work and the tool row (Expo, Ionic, Capacitor) |
| Latest | 2nd place, Cagayan Innovation Hack 2026 (link to #recognition) | Recognition / Journey |
No dates of availability, no location, no clock. The two links are quiet ghost links (hairline underline that inks on hover, trailing
arrow that nudges 3px), and the whole cell is the hit area (`::after` inset 0; the link's own box is 28px tall, 44px on touch).
Separators and the arrow are `nowrap`-bound so a wrap never strands a lone "·" or "→".
